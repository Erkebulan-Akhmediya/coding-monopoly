# Welcome to Coding Monopoly! 🎲

**Coding Monopoly** is a real-time multiplayer board game for software engineers. Solve coding problems, gain XP, roll the dice, and navigate the 32-cell board!

---

### 🎯 Objective
Be the first player to reach **500 XP** to win the match!

---

### 🔄 How Your Turn Works
1. **Choose Difficulty**:
   - 🟢 **Easy**: 30-second timer → **1 Dice Roll** on correct answer.
   - 🟡 **Medium**: 45-second timer → **2 Dice Rolls** on correct answer.
   - 🔴 **Hard**: 60-second timer → **3 Dice Rolls** on correct answer.
2. **Solve the Problem**:
   - Answer the multiple-choice or free-text question before the countdown expires.
3. **Roll & Move**:
   - **Correct**: Roll the dice and advance clockwise around the perimeter board.
   - **Incorrect or Timeout**: 0 rolls, your turn ends immediately.

---

### 🗺️ Board & Special Cells
The board has **32 perimeter cells** with unique effects:

- 🚩 **Deploy (Cell 0 / GO)**: Land directly for **+100 XP**, or pass it during a lap for a **+50 XP** lap bonus.
- 🧊 **Code Freeze (Cell 8)**: Frozen state — you enter Code Freeze (jail equivalent).
- ☕ **Coffee Break (Cell 16)**: Neutral rest zone giving a **+5 XP** boost.
- 🚨 **Deadline (Cell 24)**: High-stakes swing event! 50% chance of **+50 XP** or 50% chance of **-20 XP**.
- 📈 **XP Gain / 📉 XP Loss**: Adds or removes XP based on the cell amount.
- ⚡ **Double XP**: Multiplies your next XP gain by **2x**.
- 🎟️ **Free Pass**: Grants a CI Shield / pass ticket in inventory to protect against penalties.
- 🌀 **Teleport**: Instantly relocates your token to a key board cell.
- ⏭️ **Skip Next**: Skips your next sequential turn.
- 🏆 **Special Challenge**: Grants a **+30 to +35 XP** bonus challenge reward.
- ❓ **Mystery**: Triggers a random effect from the catalog.

---

### 👁️ Spectating
When it is another player's turn, watch the live board and standings. Problem details are kept secret from spectators to guarantee fairness.

Good luck, engineer! 🚀
